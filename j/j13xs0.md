## Goal
hug mem rescope (one memory or --from a batch) records each move in memory_fixes, so hug mem stats --fixes shows who moved which memory, from which scope to which, and when.

## Purpose
qccpka trace: two batch rescopes (2026-11-18T19:49:51Z, 58 rows; 2026-11-19T01:26:55Z, 9 rows) left no memory_fixes row; memory_fixes has 25 rows (24 forget, 1 supersede). Tracing them took journal archaeology across sessions.

## Principle
Record only; no change to what rescope moves or to its backups.
