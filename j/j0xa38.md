## Goal
Decide whether a hosted sync service is worth building

## Purpose
Jørgen listed a hosted service as a maybe: a small-fee service to view, edit and sync without self-hosting, whose point is avoiding lock-in to one company's tool (memory 2na8v3, 2026-11-06; muninn/docs/ROADMAP.md 'Maybe: hosted service'; huginn/DESIGN.md). His business intent (340bhh, 2026-11-11): he wants to be the hosting provider himself, self-hosting for own use stays free so people contribute and adopt, and he keeps the right to sell the hosted service; the aim is sustainable income with low overhead, not getting rich quick.

## Principle
Decide, do not build: the outcome is a recorded yes or no with reasons. Separate design, later: first make a great tool and use it ourselves (huginn/DESIGN.md). Self-hosting for own use stays free (340bhh), so the hosted service may only add convenience on top of it.

## Relevant details
Sync today is git: every board and the memory branch commit, fetch, rebase and push to the repo's own remote (docs/DESIGN.md 'Sync'), so a self-hosted remote already works. Licence: MPL-2.0 with a contributor agreement that keeps relicensing open (commit ce7b3ab2, 2026-11-11), which bears on selling a hosted version. Labelled 'later', priority low.
