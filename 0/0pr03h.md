## Goal
Every task's Goal is one sentence of at most 140 characters.

## Purpose
Jørgen 2026-11-19 11:26Z: the Goal must be short; AI tends to bloat and that loses focus, 'we have to fight for focus'. Board at 11:26Z: open tasks' goals median 263 chars, p90 492, max 598; 43 of 67 over 200.

## Principle
The Goal says where we aim; everything else goes to Details. Lint warns, never rewrites. Agents are told the limit where they write tasks.

## Relevant details
Lint finding goal-too-long (warning) with fix: hug task set ID --goal "one sentence" --details "...". hug task new refuses a goal over the limit with the same fix. Help for new/set, the AGENTS.md section and setup's template state it. Existing tasks: a dry-run list of proposed one-sentence goals (old text moved to Details) for crab to approve before any write. Example: 8a0ybb rewritten 11:27Z.

Sentence rule (huginn/internal/task/goal.go Sentences, conservative: when unsure, one sentence). A boundary is (a) a blank line, or (b) the last of a run of . ! ? , then optional closing ) ] " ' ” ’, then whitespace, then an uppercase letter (after optional opening ( [ " ' “ ‘ * _ backtick). A '.' is no boundary when the word it ends is an abbreviation (e.g. i.e. etc. vs. cf. no. Dr. ...), a dotted short form (U.S.), one capital (an initial) or a number (a list marker). So e.g. Foo, v0.7.85, main.go, task:abc123, 'J. Smith', '1. Do' never split; 'it works. Then' does, 'it works. then' does not. Over 140 characters (runes) also fails.
