## Goal
Agents keep no todo or todo blocker for a wait on a person; the question stays on the task and the answer notifies them.

## Purpose
Jørgen 2026-11-25 09:24Z, on crab's todo list full of blockers that copied task state: 'your instructions are not good enough' and 'When I respond, you will be notified ... That's a reliable thing'.

## Principle
One place for each fact: waits live on the task, todos are only your own next steps; finished todos are removed, not kept.
