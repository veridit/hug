## Goal
Each section in the overview and lists starts with a rule carrying its title and ends with a rule, so you see which one you move in.

## Purpose
Today sections run into each other, and it is hard to see where the focused one starts and stops.

## Principle
Same frame as a task's page (4ddcwa): ↑ and ↓ sit on the rules; one focus cue, the blinking > (q3sh69).
