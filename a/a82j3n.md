## Goal
While v waits for its second key, the underlines under the keys that can come next (o, l, b, !) blink together at the cursor rate, and there is no separate blinking cursor after 'view:'. Nothing else on the screen moves; the colours stay as they are (911j70). The v you pressed shows as picked: solid (a filled accent cell) for as long as v waits, then back to its normal look.

## Purpose
Jørgen 2026-11-19 10:26Z (crab console, screenshot of the view line after v): rather than a secondary blinking cursor, the underlines on the choices now available should blink. Replaces the slot cursor from 911j70 check 3 (0d479a96, viewline.go: m.vCursor drawn in the slot after 'view:').

## Principle
One moving element (memory y1mee5): the next keys themselves pulse. Blink phase from the existing vCursor ticks (bubbles 530 ms), no new timer. On the off phase the key letters stay in their colour without underline; on the on phase they are underlined as today. While v waits, v itself is drawn solid, the accent as a filled cell, since it is picked and changes the state (Jørgen 10:27Z); this is the one place a fill is allowed, as the cursor shape. Esc, a view key or any other key ends the blink with the last tick (as now). The answer box cursor is untouched.

## Relevant details
Code: huginn/internal/tui/viewline.go viewLine (slot, viewKey style), tui.go vCursor handling (1317-1490). Proof of blink: two v-pending frames about 0.53 s apart differ only in the underline of o l b ! (memory 7drdb1). Goldens at 100x30 and 160x45 for both phases.
