h00cs2 design proposal (hatchling, 2026-11-18)

FACTS (live store, brew v0.7.54, 03:28Z)
- dream_last_ns unset; 0 `hug mem serve`; no LaunchAgent: dream has never run.
- dream --dry-run: retire 3 duplicates, 111 judgement items (a real pass
  caps them at 10 vet + 10 agent-name + 20 ended = 40), 10.1 s, 377 MB.
- hook.log, last 7 days: 54 quiet gaps >= 30 min, at least 3 every day.

TRIGGER: the capture hook starts a detached "dream waiter"
- turn_end / session_end (capture's hook, already detached by jcode) adds
  one cheap check: dream_last_ns older than DreamEvery (20 h) and no waiter
  queued (a lock-file try, like project.Nudge). If so it spawns
  `hug mem dream --when-quiet` detached and returns. The hook's cost is one
  meta read plus one lock try.
- The waiter sleeps until QuietFor (30 min) has passed since the last
  user turn (session_turns.last_ns), then re-checks. A new turn means it
  sleeps again. It gives up after 12 h; the next turn_end starts another.
- When quiet, it runs the pass niced (nice 19, GOMAXPROCS=2) under a dream
  lock that serve's ticker also takes, so they never run twice. It sets
  dream_last_ns and logs to hook.log.
- serve keeps its ticker, now calling the same app.DreamIfDue.
- Rejected: a LaunchAgent/systemd/Task Scheduler (three installers, and it
  runs while nobody captures). Rejected: the recall hook (a person is at
  the keyboard then, and 10 s is too slow for a hook).

RETIRING STAYS PREVIEWABLE: unchanged. Only automatic-band duplicates
retire, each pass writes an undo receipt, and judgement items go to agents
at breaks (existing cue, at most once per session per 6 h).

  (or "never").
