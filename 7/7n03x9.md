## Goal
hug task resume withdraws your own open question on any task, todo or doing, and never tries to take the task.

## Purpose
Withdrawing a question is how an asker takes it back; today it fails on a todo task and refuses on a second doing task.

## Principle
Withdrawing is about the question, not about who holds the task; every refusal ends with a fix: line.
